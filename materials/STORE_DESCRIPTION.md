## Full Description

Tired of managing hundreds of snippets scattered across flat JSON files? SnippetVault brings order to the chaos.

### What You Get

🌳 **Tree-view organization** — Group snippets by language, project, or custom categories. Collapse, expand, navigate.

🔍 **Real-time search** — Type a few characters and instantly find the snippet you need, matching across prefix, description, and body text.

✏️ **Inline editing** — Click any snippet to open a clean preview/edit panel. Tweak and save without touching JSON by hand.

⚡ **One-click insert** — Hit Ctrl+Space in any file to drop your selected snippet at the cursor.

💾 **ZIP export** — Back up all your snippets (or restore from a previous export) with a single click. (ZIP import coming in Phase 2.)

### Why Local-First?

Your snippets are YOUR knowledge — your shortcuts, your patterns, your hard-won solutions. SnippetVault keeps them where they belong: on your machine. No cloud account, no subscription, no vendor lock-in.

### Perfect For

- Developers who hoard snippets across dozens of projects
- Teams that want consistent snippet libraries without central server overhead
- Privacy-conscious engineers who refuse to upload code to third-party clouds

### Zero Network Calls (Unless You Choose Otherwise)

By default SnippetVault makes no external requests. The only exception is Pro license activation — a single HTTPS POST to verify your key. Everything else is pure local file I/O.
