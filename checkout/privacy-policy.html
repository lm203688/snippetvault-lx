SnippetVault Pro — Privacy Policy Minimal data

Effective date: 2026-11-18   Applies to: SnippetVault Pro, a VS Code extension by SnapMark Studio.

1. What we collect

  • Purchase email. After you buy, we store the email you used on Paddle in a small license service, solely to confirm your purchase and deliver your license key.
  • Nothing else. SnippetVault stores your snippets entirely on your own device. We do not access, read, or store your code snippets or any project data.

2. How it is used

  • The purchase email is used only to verify your entitlement and return your license key on the delivery page.
  • It is not sold, shared, or used for marketing, and is not combined with any other data.

3. Third-party sharing

  • Payments. Checkout is handled by Paddle (a third-party payment provider) under its own privacy policy. We do not see or store your card or payment details.
  • Delivery. The delivery page calls our license service (a small HTTPS endpoint) with your email to confirm your purchase. No other data is transmitted.

4. Data retention & your rights

  • We keep the purchase email for as long as needed to provide delivery and support.
  • You may request deletion of your purchase email at any time by contacting us; this does not affect the validity of the sale.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at support@lxstudio.app.
