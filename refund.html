SnippetVault Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

1. Eligibility

You may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of purchase, provided you have not yet activated the Pro license key on any machine.

2. EU Digital Content Directive Compliance

In accordance with EU Directive 2021/2121 (amending Directive 2011/83/EU), when activating a digital product you will be asked to confirm that you acknowledge the loss of your right of withdrawal upon activation. By checking the activation consent box, you waive the 14-day cancellation right for the specific license key.

3. How to Request a Refund

Email support@lxstudio.app with:

  • Your order number (from the Paddle receipt)
  • The email address used at purchase
  • A brief reason for the refund request

We aim to respond within 3 business days. Approved refunds are processed via the original payment method within 7–10 business days.

4. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds are not available if:

  • More than 14 days have passed since purchase.
  • The Pro license key has already been activated (even partially).
  • The request is made after you have downloaded or imported exported content.

5. Free Tier

SnippetVault's free tier requires no payment and therefore has no refund policy. You may use it indefinitely within the 100-snippet limit.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

7. Contact

For refund inquiries, contact support@lxstudio.app.
